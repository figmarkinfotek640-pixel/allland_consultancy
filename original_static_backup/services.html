Frequently Asked Questions About Our Services

Answers to common questions regarding fees, documentation, and processing timelines.

Are your initial counselling sessions really 100% free?

Yes. We evaluate your academic background, backlogs, test scores, and budget without any hidden charge. You only proceed with formal processing once you are completely confident in the university and pathway selected.

How does All Land help with German Blocked Accounts?

We are official partners with leading German Federal Foreign Office approved providers including Fintiba, Expatrio, and Coracle. We assist with digital verification, money transfers via nationalized banks, and incoming health insurance coverage.

What is the German Nursing Ausbildung program?

It is a 3-year government-accredited vocational nursing apprenticeship in German hospitals. It is 100% tuition-free, pays a monthly stipend of €1,000–€1,250 during your studies, and requires German B2 proficiency. We provide German language coaching support in Kerala.

How long does certificate attestation & MEA Apostille take?

Standard MEA Apostille typically takes 3 to 7 working days, while State HRD and Embassy authentications take between 10 to 20 days depending on the state university verification timelines. We offer expedited tracking for urgent visa appointments.
