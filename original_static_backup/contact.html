Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is initial profile evaluation really 100% free?

Yes, absolutely. We evaluate your academic background, backlogs, test scores, and financial budget without charging any registration or consultation fee.

What documents should I bring to the Aluva office?

Bring copies of your 10th, 12th, and degree mark sheets, provisional certificate, passport (if available), resume, and any language test scorecards (IELTS, PTE, or German Goethe certificates).

Can I consult online if I live outside Ernakulam?

Yes. We actively counsel students across all 14 districts of Kerala and international NRIs via Google Meet, Zoom, and WhatsApp with full digital document verification.

Do you help with education loans and blocked accounts?

Yes, we guide you through opening your German blocked account (Fintiba, Expatrio, Coracle) and facilitate loan documentation with leading nationalized banks.
