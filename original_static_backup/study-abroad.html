🇩🇪 Germany • Zero Tuition Fees
Europe's #1 Economic & Tech Leader

Study in Germany — €0 Tuition Degrees

Over 400 state-funded public universities in Germany charge zero tuition fees for international students across Bachelor's and Master's programs. Enjoy unmatched research facilities, high living standards, and low semester fees.

€0 / Year Tuition (Public Unis)
18 Months Job Search Visa (PSW)
21–24 Mo Fast-Track PR
Popular Disciplines & Pathways:
Automotive Engineering Computer Science & AI Renewable Energy Data & Business Analytics Nursing Adaptation
Key Requirements: Minimum 60%+ in Bachelor's degree • APS Certificate • €11,904 Blocked Account • English Medium (MOI) or IELTS 6.5.

🇨🇦 Canada • Up to 3-Year PGWP
Top Global Immigration & Career Hub

Study in Canada — Direct Post-Grad Work Permits

Canada is renowned for welcoming international students with clear, lawful transition pathways from graduation to permanent residency. Co-op degrees allow students to earn while learning with major Canadian employers.

Up to 3 Yrs PGWP Open Work Permit
Open Work Spousal Work Permit
Bonus CRS Express Entry / PNP Points
Popular Disciplines & Pathways:
Software Engineering Healthcare & Nursing Project Management Supply Chain & Logistics PG Diplomas & Co-op
Key Requirements: Minimum 55%+ in degree • Provincial Attestation Letter (PAL) guidance • GIC deposit • IELTS 6.5 or PTE 60+.
Enquire for Canada Admission → Talk to Canada Specialist
🇬🇧 United Kingdom • 1-Year Master's
Prestigious Russell Group Academic Heritage

Study in the UK — Fast-Track 1-Year Master's

Complete your Master's degree in just 12 months instead of 2 years, saving a massive amount on tuition and living costs. Followed by a 2-year Graduate Route visa allowing you to work full-time across London and the UK.

1 Year Fast Master's Duration
2 Years Graduate Route (PSW)
NHS Direct Healthcare Sponsorships
Popular Disciplines & Pathways:
1-Year MSc & MBA NHS Nursing & Public Health Fintech & Banking Cybersecurity & Cloud International Commercial Law
Key Requirements: Minimum 55%+ in degree • IELTS waiver available with 70%+ in 12th English • 28-day funds proof • CAS issuance support.
Enquire for UK Admission → Talk to UK Specialist
🇳🇿 New Zealand • Green List Fast-Track
High Standard of Living & Direct Residence

Study in New Zealand — Green List Residence

All 8 state universities in New Zealand are ranked in the world's top 3%. New Zealand's Green List framework provides in-demand roles with direct-to-residence or fast-track permanent residency upon graduation.

Green List Direct PR Pathway
Up to 3 Yrs Post-Study Work Visa
Top 10 Global Quality of Living
Popular Disciplines & Pathways:
Civil & Construction Engineering Nursing & Healthcare IT & Cloud Infrastructure Agri-Business & Wine Tech Hospitality & Tourism
Key Requirements: Bachelor's degree • NZD 20,000 living fund proof • Partner open work visa eligible • IELTS 6.5.
